Fitness Assessment Details

Comprehensive evaluation available at Bulldogs Fitness Center — Siem Reap

Body Composition Analysis

Visbody Scan

A detailed body composition scan that provides a clear picture of your current physical baseline and helps establish starting measurements to track progress over time.

Assessment Includes:

  • Body Weight
  • Body Mass Index (BMI)
  • Body Fat Percentage (BFP)
  • Body Fat Mass
  • Lean Body Mass
  • Muscle Mass
  • Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM)
  • Body Water
  • Visceral Fat Level
  • Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR)
  • Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)
  • Metabolic Age
  • Body Composition Score

Additional Assessments:

  • Body Circumference Measurements — neck, arms, chest, waist, hips, thighs, and calves
  • Posture Analysis — reviews head position, shoulder alignment, pelvic position, and lower-body posture
  • Shoulder Function Assessment — measures shoulder range of motion and identifies potential movement limitations

Cardiorespiratory Assessment

YMCA 3-Minute Step Test

Evaluates your aerobic fitness using the YMCA 3-Minute Step Test, measuring heart-rate recovery after three minutes of stepping. The results establish your current fitness level, track progress over time, and help guide safe and effective cardiovascular training.

Assessment Includes:

  • Resting Heart Rate
  • Recovery Heart Rate
  • Estimated VO₂ Max
  • Cardiorespiratory Fitness Rating
  • Progress Tracking Baseline
