Fitness Assessment Details

Comprehensive evaluation available at Bulldogs Fitness Center — Siem Reap

Body Composition Analysis

Visbody 3D Scan

A high-precision 3D scan giving a clear physical baseline and recording detailed body metrics to track progress over time.

Assessment Includes:

  • Body Weight & Body Mass Index (BMI)
  • Body Fat Percentage (BFP) & Body Fat Mass
  • Lean Body Mass & Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM)
  • Body Water & Visceral Fat Rating
  • Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR)
  • Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) & Metabolic Age
  • Overall Body Composition Score

Additional Structural Assessments:

  • Circumference measurements: chest, arms, waist, hips, thighs
  • Posture alignment & pelvic tilt evaluation
  • Shoulder range of motion assessment

Cardiorespiratory Assessment

YMCA 3-Minute Step Test

Evaluates aerobic cardiovascular fitness by tracking recovery heart rates following three minutes of steady stepping.

Assessment Includes:

  • Resting Heart Rate
  • Heart Rate Recovery Kinetics
  • Estimated VO₂ Max Output
  • Cardiorespiratory Fitness Tier Rating
  • Personalized Aerobic Training Baseline
