Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Starting

I've never worked out in a gym before. Is this suitable?

Absolutely. Over 60% of clients begin with zero gym experience. Every session starts with movement assessment, fundamental technique calibration, and weights calibrated specifically for your comfort level.

Where do the training sessions take place?

In-person sessions take place at Bulldogs Fitness Center in Siem Reap, Cambodia, equipped with professional barbells, dumbbells, cables, and the Visbody 3D diagnostic scanner.

Do I need to follow an extreme or restrictive diet?

No. Extreme diets invariably fail long-term. We establish practical macronutrient and calorie targets that work with Khmer, Western, or vegetarian dining habits.

What should I bring to my first session?

Bring comfortable athletic wear, flat training shoes or runners, a water bottle, and a sweat towel. All training equipment is provided at the gym.
