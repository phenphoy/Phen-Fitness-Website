          Questions

          Before you message me

          The things people ask most. Anything else, send me a message.

          Is the first consultation really free?

          Yes. The initial consultation is free and takes 30 minutes. We talk through your goals, lifestyle, injuries, exercise background and schedule, and I recommend a starting pathway. There is no sales pressure.

          How much does training cost?

          Initial consultation: free. Fitness assessment: $20 ($5 for gym members). 1-on-1 personal training: $200 per month, Monday to Friday, 1-hour sessions. Small-group training for 2–3 people: $175 per person per month. Online coaching is a custom, flexible plan, so message me for a quote. There are no hidden registration fees or locked contracts.

          Where and when do you train people?

          At Bulldogs Fitness Center in Siem Reap. Hours are Monday to Thursday 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM, Friday 6:00 AM – 7:00 PM and Saturday 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM.

          I am a beginner. Can I still start?

          Yes. My coaching is built for beginners and busy professionals who want to lose fat, build lean muscle, increase energy and train with lasting confidence. Every plan starts from where you are today, with your injuries and mobility in mind.

          What happens in the fitness assessment?

          It includes a Visbody 3D body composition scan, a YMCA 3-minute step test (VO₂ max recovery) and a posture and mobility baseline, so we start from real numbers.

          How do I book, and how fast do you reply?

          Message me on Telegram or WhatsApp, or use the booking section on this page. I usually reply within 1–2 hours.

          What are your qualifications?

          I am EREPS Level 3 registered, CPR & AED certified, and I continue my education through NASM. Open the Qualifications section to see each certificate, or check my registration live on EREPS.

          Fitness assessment details

          A full evaluation at Bulldogs Fitness Center, Siem Reap.

          Body composition

          Visbody 3D scan

          A high-precision 3D scan that records detailed body metrics as a baseline to track progress.

          • Body weight & BMI
          • Body fat percentage & body fat mass
          • Lean body mass & skeletal muscle mass
          • Body water & visceral fat rating
          • Waist-to-hip ratio
          • Basal metabolic rate & metabolic age
          • Overall body composition score
          • Circumferences: chest, arms, waist, hips, thighs
          • Posture alignment & pelvic tilt
          • Shoulder range of motion
          Cardiorespiratory

          YMCA 3-minute step test

          Measures aerobic fitness by tracking your heart rate recovery after three minutes of steady stepping.

          • Resting heart rate
          • Heart rate recovery
          • Estimated VO₂ max
          • Cardiorespiratory fitness rating
          • Personal aerobic training baseline
